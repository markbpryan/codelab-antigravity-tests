Create a draft antigravity rule for experiments where I want antigravity to run through Google Codelabs, taking actions in the codelabs like a human would. I want Antigravity to not take every single detail literally but I also want to avoid it making workarounds that wouldn't occur to a journeyman user. The goal of these runs is to determine whether a reasonably skilled human could successfully follow the steps in the codelab. The reasons for a human not being able to successfully complete a codelab could, for example, be an error in the codelab, a bug in the function of the product that is being exercised in the codelab, or a detail that is missing in the codelab that wouldn't be obvious to a reasonably skilled human user trying to work through the codelab.
